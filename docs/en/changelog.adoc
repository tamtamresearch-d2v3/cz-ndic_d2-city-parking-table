== Documentation change history

// Documentation revision (MAJOR.MINOR.REVISION). Format changes see CHANGELOG.adoc in the specification.
=== 1.0.0

* New documentation of the `cz-ndic_d2-city-parking-table` format version 1.0 (DATEX II 3.7). The format is new;
  it replaces the static part of the information on P+R car parks from the situation publication
  (`CarParks` in the cz-ndic_d2-parking-occupancy-v1.0 format, DATEX II 2.3).
* Changes compared to the previous format:
** The format is converted to the DATEX II 3.7 model. The `d2LogicalModel` and `exchange` envelopes
   are not used; the root is `d2:payload`, the format is identified by the `@profileName` and `@profileVersion` attributes.
** The location of the car park is directly in the table (`loc:SingleRoadLinearLocation` – OpenLR, Global Network, ALERT-C,
   location path and coordinates for display); predefined locations are not referenced. The car park is described
   by a point and the road section on which the point lies.
** The identifier of the car park is the GUID under which NDIC publishes the car park; previous identifiers
   (e.g. `PR-2`) are not used.
** The publication contains several tables by type of car park: `CzechParkAndRideTable` (P+R)
   and `CzechCityParkingTable` (other city car parks).
** The profile is narrowed for city car parks: only the level `place`, without the motorway and truck parking
   scenarios (`truckParking`, `motorwayParking`, `nearbyMotorwayParking`, `restArea`, `serviceArea`), without
   OpenLR in the opposite direction and without unknown opening times.
* Capacity, operator, opening times and display coordinates are optional; they are not given for car parks where
  they are not known.
* The sample `pr-table.xml` is converted from NDIC production data (1 Oct 2026).
