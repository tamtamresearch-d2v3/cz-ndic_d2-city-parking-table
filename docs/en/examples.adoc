== Message examples

The samples are part of the specification package `{schema-uri}.zip` (folder `samples`).

=== pr-table.xml – car park tables

A real state from NDIC data (1 Oct 2026): 11 car parks in Prague in two tables – `CzechParkAndRideTable`
(9 P+R car parks) and `CzechCityParkingTable` (2 city car parks).

[source,xml]
----
include::{samplesdir}/pr-table.xml[tags=**]
----

Envelope and header::
Root `d2:payload` of the type `prk:ParkingTablePublication` with the attributes `@profileName` and `@profileVersion`,
`publicationTime` and `publicationCreator`. `feedType` refers to the distribution (data source).

Car parks::
* Each car park has a GUID identifier, a name and level 0 (`layer`, `type` = `place`).
* P+R car parks have the P+R usage (`operatingPattern` of the type `prk:UsageScenario` with `type` =
  `parkAndRide`).
* The data do not yet give the capacity, the operator or the opening times.

Location::
* The car park is a point (`coordinatesForDisplay`) on a section of the car park road. The section is described by OpenLR,
  the Global Network and the location path `gmlLineString`.
* For most car parks one Global Network section is enough. The point of the P+R Opatov car park lies on the boundary
  of two sections, so the location gives both.
* The Muzeum car park also has an ALERT-C location.
